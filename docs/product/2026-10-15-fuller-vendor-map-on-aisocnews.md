# A fuller, cleaner vendor map on aisocnews.com
_15 October 2026 · aisocnews.com and the other Market Monitor sites (Maturity Map, vendor lists)_

## What shipped
- The smallest vendors now appear on the Maturity Map. Before, they could not be placed at all.
- Vendors no longer fall off the map because their LinkedIn page stayed the same or changed its address.
- Funding figures from a different company with the same name no longer come back after we remove them.
- New vendors are searched on Bluesky, X and Reddit from the day they are added.
- UnderDefense joined the AI in the SOC market. Every vendor that asked to be listed has now been told where to find itself.

## Why it matters

**Small vendors are on the map.** LinkedIn shows the smallest companies only as a size range, "2-10 employees", with no exact count. The map needs a count, so these vendors were tracked but never placed. Two of the newest AI SOC startups, NextSOC and Vinci Logic, were in this position. Now the map uses the middle of the range, and both appear in the Emerging stage. Readers looking for early-stage vendors, the ones they are least likely to have heard of, now find them. Vendor pages still show the range LinkedIn gave, not a number it didn't give.

**Vendors stay on the map.** Two separate faults removed vendors that had done nothing wrong. A vendor whose headcount didn't change looked out of date and dropped off, which is how HTCD went missing. A vendor that renamed its LinkedIn page had its new readings thrown away, which is how Intezer went missing. On 15 October the map rated 96 AI SOC vendors, up from 91 the day before.

**Funding is the right company's.** Our guess at a vendor's Crunchbase page sometimes lands on a different company with the same name. On 9 October we removed four such matches: a charting tool, a blockchain firm, a hygiene brand and a housing community. The next collection put them straight back. Now a removed match stays removed. Separately, we corrected Variance's funding from $3.6M to $26M after its March Series A.

**New vendors are complete from day one.** Adding a vendor used to set up news searches but not social ones, so a new vendor's posts on X and Bluesky were missed. Adding a vendor now sets up both.

**Vendors that asked were told.** Five vendors that asked to be listed, and were added weeks ago, had never heard back. Each has now had an email saying where it sits on the map, with a working link.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- The Maturity Map now places the smallest vendors, using the size range LinkedIn gives them.
- Vendors no longer drop off the map when their LinkedIn page is unchanged or renamed.
- Funding data from a company that only shares a vendor's name stays out once removed.
- New vendors are searched on social media from the day they are added.
- UnderDefense added to the AI in the SOC market.

## Positioning notes
A market map is judged first on whether it lists the vendor the reader is looking for. Early-stage vendors are the ones a reader most wants to discover, and before this change they were the ones the map could not show. Leaving out vendors that had done nothing wrong, and showing another company's funding, are the errors most likely to cost us a vendor's trust. Vendors check their own entry, so these fixes also protect our standing with the vendors who send us submissions.

## Limits and what's next
- A small vendor's position uses an estimate, 6 staff for "2-10". Two vendors in the same range look the same size until LinkedIn gives a count.
- Ranges above 50 staff are not estimated. A larger vendor with no exact count still stays off the map. We have not seen one yet.
- Intezer was still off the map at the end of the day, waiting for a fresh LinkedIn reading.
- SOCAI has no LinkedIn page, so it cannot be placed. We have asked the company for one.
- The audit lists 11 AI SOC vendors missing from one of the two social searches. Five were removed on purpose because they matched unrelated posts, and one is a false flag. The other five need a decision.
- eSentire asked to be listed but is a managed security service rather than an AI SOC product. It stays off unless Oliver decides otherwise.
