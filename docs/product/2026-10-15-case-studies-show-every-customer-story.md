# Case studies show every customer story
_15 October 2026 · aisocnews.com, the Case studies section_

## What shipped
- The Case studies section now lists every customer story a vendor publishes, whether or not it names the customer.

## Why it matters

**The section was nearly empty.** Before, Case studies only took stories where the vendor kept the customer anonymous. Stories that named the customer went into Market moves instead. Over the last 30 days that left one case study on the page, Nebulock's insider risk story about "a global software company". The other eight customer stories in the same period were spread through Market moves.

**Now the customer evidence sits in one place.** Over the same 30 days the section holds all nine stories, including Solarig standardising on CrowdStrike, Cole, Scott & Kissane using 7AI, and EQUANS building its SOC with Torq. A buyer looking for who actually uses these products, or a vendor checking how its customer wins compare, finds them together.

**Named and anonymous stories stay distinct.** Each story carries a tag: "Customer" when the vendor names the customer, "Case study" when it doesn't. A named customer is stronger evidence, and the tag lets readers weigh that.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- The Case studies section on aisocnews.com now includes customer stories that name the customer, not only anonymous ones. Each story is tagged "Customer" or "Case study".

## Demo / walkthrough
1. Open https://aisocnews.com/?days=30.
2. Scroll to Case studies. The front page shows the three newest stories.
3. Open the section's own page to see all of them, each tagged "Customer" or "Case study".

## Positioning notes
Customer evidence is what buyers trust most in a crowded market, and it is the hardest thing to find across vendor sites. Gathering it in one section makes it one of the page's most useful parts.

## Limits and what's next
- Government contract awards, such as Method Security's Space Force award, stay in Market moves. They are contracts, not customer stories.
- Market moves no longer shows customer stories, so it carries deals, funding, partnerships, leadership changes and incidents.
- The change is on aisocnews.com only. The other market sites still file named customers under Market moves.
- Live on aisocnews.com since 18:04 on 15 October.
