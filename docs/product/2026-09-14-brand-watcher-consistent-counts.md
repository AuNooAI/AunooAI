# Brand Watcher: the cards now agree with the article list
_2026-09-14 · Brand Watcher dashboard, Articles tab, Comparison tab_

## What shipped

- Category Distribution, the category chips on the Articles tab, Category Comparison,
  Share of Voice and the monthly volume chart now count only the news articles the
  Articles list shows. A brand's own LinkedIn posts and its Glassdoor reviews stay on the
  Social tab and no longer inflate these cards.
- Clicking a category chip now opens as many articles as the chip promised.
- The Category Comparison total and the Share of Voice count are now the same number for
  every brand, because both count each article once. An article that carries several
  categories still appears in each of its category columns.
- The sentiment bar on the dashboard, the weekly sentiment line and the Sentiment Breakdown
  card count each article once, so their totals match the "News articles" figure.

## Why it matters

A customer reviewing the Oviva site saw 104 articles in one card, 95 in another, 20 in a
third and 13 in the list, all for the same brand and window. Numbers that disagree with
each other cost more trust than a smaller number would, because the reader cannot tell
which one to believe. Every card now draws from the same definition of "an article about
this brand": on-brand (relevance at or above 0.4), a real news item rather than the brand's
own channel, and counted once.

## What did not change

- The taxonomy still allows an article to carry several categories. The category chips
  therefore add up to more than the article count when that happens (on Oviva, 15 chips
  for 13 articles). The card title says so.
- The Social tab is unchanged. LinkedIn posts and Glassdoor reviews are still collected and
  still shown there.

## Where it is live

oviva, wiley and wileytest, deployed 14 September 2026.
