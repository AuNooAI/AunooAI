# aisocnews.com MCP access now costs $139 a month
_2026-10-08 · aisocnews.com, the public AI-in-the-SOC market report; Stripe checkout_

## What changed

**MCP access dropped from $279 to $139 a month.** It is still the only thing aisocnews.com
sells. It gives a customer's AI tools (Claude, ChatGPT or their own agents) a key to our MCP
server, so they can query the market's articles, posts, vendor profiles and sentiment directly
instead of reading them off a page. The site itself stays free to read.

The new price shows everywhere a buyer meets it: the subscribe page, the line in the market
page's contact panel, and Stripe Checkout.

## Who is affected

No one is billed differently, because no one was paying the old price. The $279 price is
archived in Stripe, so it can no longer be bought.

## For sales and marketing

- Quote **$139 a month** for MCP access. Older notes from 2026-09-09 and 2026-09-22 carry the
  earlier prices and say so at the top.
- The pitch has not changed: the data is free to read, and what people pay for is not having to
  read it, because their AI tools can query it.
