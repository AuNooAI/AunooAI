# Every AI SOC vendor now tracked on X, Reddit and Bluesky
_2026-10-08 · aisocnews.com, Social panel and vendor pages_

## What shipped
- We now search for every vendor on aisocnews.com by name on Bluesky, X and Reddit, not just 46 of them on Bluesky.

## Why it matters
**Before:** we searched for only 46 of the 116 vendors by name, and only on Bluesky. On X and
Reddit we searched for eight general phrases such as "AI SOC". A post that named a vendor but
not one of those phrases never reached the site. As a result, 66 vendors had no social posts
from those platforms at all. Those 66 included IBM, Rapid7, ServiceNow and Trellix.

**Now:** we search for every vendor by name on all three platforms once a day. The first X and
Reddit run brought in 625 new posts. Our AI scoring step judged 273 of them relevant to the AI
SOC market. 102 were linked to 25 vendors on the same day.

**Who notices:** readers comparing vendors. A smaller or newer vendor's social activity now
shows up next to the large firms, where before it was missing.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- aisocnews.com now follows what is said about every tracked vendor on X, Reddit and Bluesky.
- Vendor pages and the Social panel include posts that name the vendor, even when the post does
  not use market terms like "AI SOC".

## Demo / walkthrough
Open aisocnews.com and go to the Social panel, or to a vendor such as Exabeam or Splunk. Posts
from X and Reddit dated 8 October onwards come from the new searches. The Bluesky additions
first run on 9 October.

## Positioning notes
Coverage of the whole vendor list, small firms included, is what separates a market tracker
from a news feed. Readers often ask about the newer AI SOC vendors, and this closes the gap
there.

## Limits and what's next
- **Wrong-company matches, checked across every vendor.** Eleven vendors whose names are also
  ordinary phrases were picking up posts about something else. Examples include airport
  security checks ("pre security"), "full-spectrum security", advice to "embed security" in
  software, Spring Security bugs ("method security"), a romance novel and a streamer's
  bodyguards. We fixed all eleven the same day and removed 55 wrong posts from their pages. New
  vendors with phrase-like names will need the same check when they are added.
- **Cost.** Each search on X and Reddit is a paid call, and each post costs one AI rating. The
  six common-word names bring in many posts that the scoring step then throws away.
- **LinkedIn gaps remain.** SOCAI has no LinkedIn page on file. Tencycle's page address looks
  misspelled. Six vendors, Trellix among them, have never had a LinkedIn post collected. We
  haven't fixed any of this.
- **Bluesky is not verified yet.** The new Bluesky names first run on 9 October.
