# Social posts show what was written, and what it means
_2026-09-14 · Social tab, social report_

## What shipped

A social post that was not written in English now shows both texts. The English
translation reads first, as before, and a "Translated · show original" line under it
opens the post exactly as the author wrote it. The social report prints the original
under the translation too. Posts written in English are unchanged.

## Why it matters

A brand team reading German, French or Japanese posts about their brand needs the
author's own words for tone, slang and anything a translation flattens, and the English
for speed. Until today only the English was on screen, so a reader could not tell a
translated post from a native one, or check a translation that read oddly.

oviva, sunstar, wiley and wileytest, deployed 14 September 2026.
