# Voices separates paper links from what researchers say
_2026-09-30 · Brand Watcher Voices on the Wiley site (wileytest)_

## What shipped
- The Wiley Voices panel has a new row, Paper shares. It holds posts that are only a paper's
  title and a link, with nothing said about the paper.
- Researchers now counts only the posts where a researcher says something about Wiley's work.
- The "posts not yet classified" note is gone. The last three posts without an audience have one.

## Why it matters
**Before, Researchers was mostly links.** Over the last 30 days the row held 386 posts. 286 of them
were a researcher pasting a Wiley paper's title and link, the text a journal's share button
writes. Those posts say nothing, so the row's sentiment sat at +4, which read as "researchers are
lukewarm about Wiley". That reading was an artefact of the counting, not an opinion.

**Now the two signals are apart.** Researchers holds 81 posts where someone comments, praises or
questions, and its sentiment is +12. Paper shares holds 340 posts and shows how widely Wiley papers
get passed around. The analyst can read opinion and reach separately instead of one diluting the
other.

**Authors are unchanged.** An author posting a link to their own paper still counts under Authors &
editors. That row is about people talking about their own work with Wiley, and a bare link from the
author is part of that story.

Every post keeps its original audience label underneath. Nothing was deleted or relabelled in the
data.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- Voices: posts that only share a paper's title and link now appear in their own Paper shares row.
- Voices: Researchers now reflects what researchers say about Wiley's publications, not how often
  they share a link.
- Voices: every post in the window now has an audience.

## Demo / walkthrough
Brand Watcher, Voices, brand Wiley, last 30 days. The columns are Authors & editors, Researchers,
Libraries, Readers, Students & teachers and Sellers. Paper shares and Competitors are rows below
the columns. Open Paper shares to see the links; open Researchers to see the commentary.

## Positioning notes
Social listening tools usually count every mention as an opinion, which inflates neutral volume. For
a publisher, most mentions are link shares. Separating them lets us report reach and opinion as two
different measures, which is the honest reading of the data.

## Limits and what's next
- The rule recognises the share-button text of Wiley Online Library, ScienceDirect, SAGE Journals,
  Taylor & Francis Online and SpringerLink. A share from another platform, or a post with only a
  bare link and no title, stays in its author's row.
- A comment written before a paper title may still be read as a bare share if the whole post is
  short. The hand check found titles in the moved posts and comments in the kept ones, but we did
  not label every post.
- Elsevier's panel barely changes (19 paper shares in 30 days), so this is mostly a Wiley effect.
- Only the Wiley site has this. Other sites use different audience sets and did not ask for it.
