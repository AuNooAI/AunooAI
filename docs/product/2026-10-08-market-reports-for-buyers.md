# Market reports now speak to the buyer
_2026-10-08 · aisocnews.com Consensus and Three Horizons pages; the reports behind them_

## What shipped
- The Consensus and Three Horizons reports on aisocnews.com are now written for a security
  team buying AI for its SOC.
- The advice in those reports is for the buyer: what to test, what to ask vendors, what to
  decide and when.
- Both reports now refresh every month, as they were meant to.
- Dates in the reports start from today, so they no longer predict things that have already
  happened.

## Why it matters
**The reports were written for the publisher, not the reader.** The AI writes each report for
one organisation. For aisocnews.com that organisation was the publisher, so its advice
sections read as the publisher's business plan: revenue from licensing, sponsored content, and
a sale of the business. A buyer who read that learned they were the product. That undercuts
the independence a market site needs. Now the organisation is an enterprise security team, so
the same sections give advice a reader can act on. For example: "Model total cost of
ownership for Microsoft ISOC including non-Microsoft data ingestion premiums", and "Require
vendor documentation of credential isolation architecture".

**The reports had never refreshed.** We meant to make a new report each month so readers can
see how the market's view changes. A fault stopped that from ever running, so the pages still
showed the 6 September reports. The first automatic refresh ran on 8 October.

**Milestones were dated in the past.** The AI copied example years from its instructions. A
report made in October 2026 listed "2025" milestones, which reads as stale. The instructions
now carry today's date, and every milestone in the new reports is 2026 or later.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- The Consensus and Three Horizons reports on aisocnews.com are now written for buyers of AI
  SOC technology, with advice on evaluation, pilots and vendor questions.
- Both reports now refresh monthly.
- Report milestones and timelines start from the current year.

## Demo / walkthrough
- Consensus: aisocnews.com → menu → Consensus. Open any category to see Strategic
  Implications, Key Decision Windows and Key Milestones, all written for a buyer.
- Three Horizons: aisocnews.com → menu → Three horizons. Each summary card ends with
  "Your window", which lists the buyer's actions for the next 0-6 and 6-18 months.

## Positioning notes
An independent market site is worth reading only if readers trust it is not working an angle.
These reports now say what the coverage supports and what a buyer should do about it. They
no longer say anything about the publisher's own commercial plans.

## Limits and what's next
- The September reports are no longer listed, because we wrote them for the publisher. The
  run history now starts on 8 October.
- Only aisocnews.com has this change. Other customer sites that publish these reports have
  neither the buyer-advice setting nor the date fix.
- A market without a reader profile set does not refresh its reports. EV Battery Cells is in
  that state.
- The Three Horizons chart in the operator app still uses a fixed 2025-2040 axis, so scenarios
  that end in 2041 draw slightly past its right edge. The public page is not affected.
