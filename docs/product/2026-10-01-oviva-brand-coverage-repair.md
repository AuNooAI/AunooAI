# Oviva's press coverage is counted again, and the competitor set is complete
_2026-10-01 · Brand Watcher, Market Monitor and the brand tools in Claude, on the Oviva site_

## What shipped
- Articles that mention Oviva are kept even when Oviva is not their main subject: a quoted
  dietitian, the CEO on a TV panel, a patient's complaint.
- Each of those articles now shows the sentence where Oviva is named.
- Posts about the OVIVA antibiotics trial no longer count as Oviva.
- Recipe-site pages no longer count as WeightWatchers press coverage.
- Noom's own blog no longer appears as Oviva news.
- Articles that arrive days after they were published now reach the article list and the
  stats. Before, they appeared only in the perception scores.
- HelloBetter and zanadio are now monitored competitors in Oviva's market, alongside the other
  seven.
- A daily check now looks for these problems and emails the team when it finds one.

## Why it matters
**Oviva looked like it had no press coverage.** Reports built on the Oviva site said Aunoo found
no earned news about Oviva in 90 days. That was wrong. We were throwing the coverage away because
each article was judged on whether it was about Oviva. A story about weight-loss injections that
quotes an Oviva dietitian is not about Oviva, but it is coverage. Now Oviva has 19 earned press
articles in the last 90 days instead of none. They include the CEO's TV booking, an award
shortlist, an investor portfolio note and a reader stuck on Oviva's NHS waiting list.

**The evidence was invisible, so readers doubted it.** Oviva's name usually sits deep in the
article body. The article list only looked at headlines and summaries, so it showed no matching
keyword, and a reader concluded the articles did not mention Oviva at all. Each article now
carries the sentence that names Oviva.

**Competitors' numbers did not line up.** Voy showed five press items in its perception score and
none in its article list. Articles that our sources deliver days after publication were never
sorted into categories, and the article list and stats only show sorted articles. That is fixed
for every brand. We also now count competitor mentions the way we count Oviva's: Voy, Numan and
Juniper picked up 13 more articles, such as Voy's clinical director quoted in The Sun and Numan's
research in the Daily Mail.

**A trial with the same name counted as a clinician's opinion.** OVIVA is also a well-known
antibiotics trial. Doctors who cited it showed up as clinicians talking about Oviva. Those posts
are now recognised as a different subject.

**A fan recipe site made WeightWatchers look better covered than it is.** One recipe site
supplied most of WeightWatchers' "press". With it removed, WeightWatchers' article count in the
brand tools went from 44 to 17, which is the real figure to compare Oviva against.

**Two competitors were missing from the market.** HelloBetter and zanadio were tracked as brands
but had no place in Oviva's market. They now have LinkedIn, Crunchbase, website and Instagram
monitoring like the rest, so the market map and vendor comparisons cover all nine.

**These problems kept coming back because nothing looked for them.** The existing quality check
only read headlines and short summaries, and it had never run on this site. The new daily check
reads the stored data and emails the team when coverage is dropped, when a same-name subject
slips in, when one site dominates a brand's press, or when a feed is filed under the wrong brand.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- Brand Watcher now counts articles that mention your brand, not only articles about it, and
  shows the sentence where the brand is named.
- Articles delivered late by a source now appear in the article list and statistics.
- Same-name subjects, such as a clinical trial sharing the brand's name, are kept out.
- Fan and recipe sites can be excluded from a brand's press coverage.
- Competitor blogs found by the market monitor are filed under the right brand.
- HelloBetter and zanadio added to the Oviva market.

## Demo / walkthrough
- Brand Watcher, brand Oviva, last 90 days: the news cards and media score carry articles,
  including the Sat.1 panel, the EY shortlist and the heise piece on electronic patient records.
- In Claude, asking for Oviva's brand articles returns each one with the sentence naming Oviva.
- Market Monitor, Oviva market, Vendors: nine vendors, including HelloBetter (Berlin, 130 staff)
  and zanadio.

## Positioning notes
Brand monitoring tools that only count articles about a company undercount small and mid-sized
brands, whose coverage is mostly quotes and mentions. Counting mentions, while screening out
same-name subjects, gives a truer picture of share of voice against larger competitors.

## Limits and what's next
- This is on the Oviva site only. Other sites have not asked for it.
- A brand needs a short list of words that usually appear near its name, such as "app" or "NHS".
  Second Nature has no list, because its name is an everyday phrase, so its mentions are not
  counted this way.
- zanadio has no LinkedIn page of its own. Its LinkedIn figures come from Sidekick Health, the
  group that runs it (209 staff, Reykjavík), so its headcount and posts are the group's.
- Neither HelloBetter nor zanadio has a public job board, so job listings stay empty for both.
- Perception still counts a few more articles than the article list for some brands: articles
  our categoriser gave no category.
- Some kept mentions are thin, such as a co-founder's past employer named in a press release.
  They count as coverage, not as stories about Oviva.
- A new same-name subject or fan site needs adding to the brand's lists. The daily check flags
  the patterns, but a person decides.
