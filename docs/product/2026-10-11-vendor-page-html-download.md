# Save a vendor page as a web page
_2026-10-11 · Market Monitor vendor page, on bugfixing_

## What shipped
- The vendor page's Download line now offers **HTML** beside MD, CSV and PDF.
- The saved file looks like the page on screen, with the same panels, colours and wording.

## Why it matters
**Before, a saved vendor page did not look like the page.** The Markdown, CSV and PDF downloads
carry the same data, but as plain text or a printed report. An analyst who wanted to send a
colleague or a client "the Exaforce page" had to take screenshots, or ask them to log in.

**Now the file is the page.** It opens in any browser with nothing to install and no login, and
it works offline. It shows:
- the current profile, with the source and date of each field;
- the vendor's events, and whether anyone other than the vendor confirmed them;
- the benchmark against the rest of the market, with the same bars as on screen;
- the headcount trend as a chart;
- funding and investors;
- announcements, site changes, open roles, LinkedIn posts and press coverage.

The file follows the reader's light or dark setting, as the app does.

Who feels it:
- **Analysts** can attach a vendor page to an email or a shortlist pack.
- **Buyers who receive it** see a source and a date on every figure, the same as an analyst in
  the app.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- New: download any vendor page as HTML. The file looks like the page in the app and opens in any
  browser, offline.
- It includes the vendor's profile with sources, events, the market benchmark, the headcount
  trend, funding, announcements, open roles and coverage.

## Demo / walkthrough
1. Open Market Monitor, pick a market and open a vendor, for example Exaforce in AI in the SOC.
2. Next to the vendor's name, click **HTML** on the Download line.
3. Open the saved file. Its panels follow the same order as the screen.

## Positioning notes
A shortlist pack usually means screenshots or a PDF with the sources stripped out. This file keeps
the source and date on every figure, and the person receiving it needs no account.

## Limits and what's next
- **It is a snapshot.** It shows the data on the day it was saved and does not update.
- **The controls are left out.** Fetch now, adding a PitchBook or ZoomInfo URL, and the Market
  Horizon weights can't work from a saved file.
- **The benchmark covers the last 30 days, as on screen.** The MD, CSV and PDF downloads use 90
  days, so their benchmark figures can differ from the HTML file's.
- **It needs a login to download, but not to read.** Anyone who receives the file can read all of
  it. That includes the benchmark's top-20 lists, which name other vendors and their figures.
  Check those before sending the file outside the team.
- **Only bugfixing has it.** Other customer sites would need the change copied and their UI
  rebuilt.
