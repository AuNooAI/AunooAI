# The aisocnews.com analyst call now costs $400 for 30 minutes and $700 for 60
_2026-09-14 · aisocnews.com "Schedule an inquiry" page_

## What shipped
- A 30 minute call with Oliver costs $400. A 60 minute call costs $700. They were $250 and $450.

## Why it matters
The page sells an ex-Gartner analyst who named the SOAR category. The old price sat at the top of
what freelance security consultants charge and well under what expert networks charge for the
same kind of call, so it undercut the credentials the page leads with. Vendors and investors, the
likeliest buyers, already pay expert networks $750 to $1,500 an hour. Practitioners still get a
price far below a Gartner seat. No calls had been sold at the old price, so nobody was affected.

## Limits and what's next
