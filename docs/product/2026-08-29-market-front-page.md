# The AI in the SOC market, as a front page
_2026-08-29 · Market Monitor public report (aisoc.aunoo.ai, now also aisocnews.com)_

## What shipped
- A front page for the market: a lead story and the period's highlights (each a headline
  that opens to its evidence), then sections for market moves, product launches, hiring,
  case studies, thought leadership and social, with the Market Horizon, the numbers, the most active
  vendors, who moved and the weekly briefing beside them.
- Each section as a page of its own, listing everything in it for the period.
- The front page is now what https://aisoc.aunoo.ai/ and https://aisocnews.com/ open.
  The existing report is the "Analyst View", one click away, and links back.
- A dark site with each page as one light card on it.
- In the shared view nothing on the front page is blurred: the hiring top five and the
  market figures are readable. The vendor list is still the ten most active.

## Why it matters
Before, the public report was one long column. A reader who wanted to know what launched this
month, or which vendors are hiring, had to scan every development in one list. Now each kind
of development has its own place on the page, and its own page when the reader wants all of
it. The lead is the most important development of the period; the sidebar keeps the map and
the findings in view.

Nothing new is being judged. The sections are the same developments the report already
found, sorted by what they are. The thought-leadership section is new to the reader but not
to the system: the opinion and research posts from vendors and practitioners were being
collected and then left out of the report.

Customer stories are labelled honestly. A story where the vendor names the customer is a
"Customer" and counts as a market move. A story about an unnamed customer is a "Case study"
and sits in its own section. The reader can tell a confirmed deployment from marketing copy.

The shared view keeps the report's rule on names — the ten most active vendors — and the
page refuses to render rather than name a vendor the reader may not see. Unlike the report,
it blurs nothing on the front page; the trial's extra is the rest of the hiring list, the
full vendor roster and the analyst evidence.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- New front page for the AI in the SOC market: lead story, then Market moves, Product
  launches, Hiring, Case studies and Thought leadership, with the Market Horizon in the

## Demo / walkthrough
1. Open https://aisocnews.com/ or https://aisoc.aunoo.ai/. "Analyst View", top right, is the
   existing report.
2. The lead story and the highlights are on the left — click a highlight to open its
   evidence; the Horizon, the numbers, the most active vendors, who moved and the briefing on
   the right. Hiring shows the five biggest recruiters; "All N →" lists every one.
3. Scroll to the sections. Click "All N →" on any section, or a section name in the top bar,
   to see the whole list for the period.
4. The small Horizon names the eight largest and fastest vendors; hover any dot to see its
   vendor; "Full map with names" opens the large map on the report.
5. Switch 7 / 30 / 90 days; the choice carries across the front page, its sections, the report
   and the news river.

## Positioning notes
The front page reads like a trade publication for one market, generated from evidence with
every item linked to its sources. That is the difference from a newsletter: nothing here is
written by hand, and every story says whose word it rests on.

## Limits and what's next
- The sections are only as good as the classification behind them. The customer split rests
  on whether the review pass recorded a customer name; a post that names a customer in a way
  the review missed shows as a case study.
- Thought leadership covers vendor LinkedIn posts, practitioner posts and research papers.
  Opinion pieces from news outlets are not classified today and do not appear there.
- The front page shows the top few items per section; the counts on the "All N →" links are
  the full figures.
- Only the AI in the SOC market has a front page; the layout is ready for any market the
  Market Monitor tracks.
