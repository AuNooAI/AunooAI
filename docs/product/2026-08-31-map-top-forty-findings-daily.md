# Every vendor on the map, forty at a time; Findings current every day
_2026-08-31 · Market Monitor: the Market Maturity Map on aisocnews.com and the Analyst View; the Findings view; the observer agent's daily mail_

## What shipped

- **80 of 85 vendors are on the map**, up from 39. A vendor no longer needs a published
  funding total to be placed; where a total exists it still counts.
- **The map opens on the top 40** by scale and momentum, with a switch to show all 80. Labels
  sit beside their dots in both views. The small map in the front-page sidebar shows the same
  forty.
- **The marker that said "funded" now says "raised in the last year"**, which is what it
  measures: a round in the last 365 days, not a vendor that has ever raised.
- **Findings update daily again.** They had stopped on 24 August. Sixty-six findings now
  cover the last 30 days, through 30 August, and the pass that produces them runs every day
  after the post review.
- **The daily observer report reaches its reader.** The SOC Automation Market Watch agent
  ran every morning but had nobody to mail; it does now, and today's report was sent.
- Registry housekeeping: Wirespeed added at a founder's request, Zaun removed, Wirespeed's
  ownership (Coalition, November 2025) recorded in its analyst note.

## Why it matters

Half the market was missing from the map for a reason a reader could not see: 42 vendors
have never published a funding total, and the map refused to place anyone without one. Now
they are placed on what is public, headcount, followers, customers, launches, hiring, and the
map says so beside the input. The cost is honest too: a vendor with no total is placed on
size and reach alone, so a small, well-followed company can sit ahead of a funded one.

Eighty dots on one arc is unreadable, which is why the map opens on the forty largest and
fastest and shows the rest on request. The first version of the switch placed labels for all
eighty and then hid half the dots, so the labels that remained pointed across the arc at
nothing; the labels are now placed for each view.

Findings are the part of the product that says "what changed" rather than "what was
published". They had gone quiet for a week without anyone noticing, because the step that
writes them had only ever been run by hand. A reader comparing the Findings tab with the
front page would have seen the page moving and the findings not.

## Positioning notes

The map is the page's one original claim, our placement rather than an analyst's, and it now
covers the market rather than its funded half. The "raised in the last year" wording matters
for the same reason: a marker a reader can check is worth more than one that sounds good.

## Limits and what's next

- The small map in the front-page sidebar also opens on the top 40 and says so; it has no
  switch of its own, the full map's is one link away.
- A vendor with no published funding total is placed without that input, which favours
  headcount and followers. The weights table says so, but a reader who skips it will not know.
- Wirespeed is rated as an independent although Coalition owns it; that was a deliberate
  call (the brand still trades as itself) and is written in its analyst note.
- Findings come from the vendors' own posts, job postings, profile and site changes. Two
  extractors (earned coverage, social spikes) are still off by design until they have a
  baseline.
- Mail delivery cannot be confirmed from the server (the Resend key is send-only); the reader
  has to say whether it arrived.
