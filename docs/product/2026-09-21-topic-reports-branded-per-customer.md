# Topic reports now carry the customer's own name
_2026-09-21 · Topic reports: the slide deck, the Word document and the web version_

## What shipped
Topic reports are addressed to the customer reading them. The cover carries their brand line,
the calibration slide names their organisation, and the copy describes their sector.

## Why it matters
Before today, every topic report on every customer site was addressed to Wiley. The name was
written into the code when the first deck was built for Wiley, and the two later formats copied
it. A Sunstar reader opening their own foresight report saw "WILEY HORIZONS" on the cover, a slide
headed "How Analysis Is Calibrated to Wiley", and a claim that the analysis is framed for
scientific publishing. Sunstar sells oral care products.

That is nine separate places in one 78-slide deck, and it was in the Word and web versions too.
It is the kind of error that costs a deal on sight, because it says the report was made for
somebody else.

The report now reads its own name from the customer profile the analysis is already based on. No
extra setup: the profile that decides how the analysis is framed now also decides how the report
is addressed. Sunstar's deck reads "How Analysis Is Calibrated to Sunstar" and "Relevant to
consumer health / oral care" without anyone configuring anything.

One smaller thing came with it. The team slide used to explain the analysts' background in terms
of academic fraud — paper mills, fake reviewers, citation rings. That lands for a publisher and
means nothing to anyone else. It now describes the same expertise as coordinated campaigns and
astroturfing unless the customer really is in publishing.

## Release notes (copy-ready)
- Topic reports are branded to your organisation: cover, calibration slide and sector wording all
  come from your own profile.
- The slide describing how the analysis is calibrated now names your organisation and your sector.
- The analyst background slide describes the manipulation patterns relevant to your industry.
- Fixed a duplicated brand name in report footers.

## Demo / walkthrough
Open Trend Convergence, generate or download a topic report, and look at slide 1, slide 7 and the
footer. The same branding appears in the Word and web downloads of the same report.

## Positioning notes
This closes an objection rather than opening an opportunity. A prospect who sees another
customer's name on their own report concludes the product is a template with their logo dropped
on it. Correct addressing is table stakes for anything sold as tailored analysis, and it is the
first thing a buyer checks.

## Limits and what's next
The branding comes from the customer profile marked as the default. A site with no default
profile gets wording that names nobody, which is correct but plain, so setting the profile is
worth doing.

Eleven other customer sites carry this report code and have not been updated yet: abbott, abm,
bwtemplate, ibaset, interroll, oviva, panaya, pbm, pearson, sage and wbm. A topic report generated
on any of them today would still be addressed to Wiley. Copying the fix across is a few minutes of
work per site.

Wiley's own site cannot build the slide deck at all — a library is missing from its environment.
That is unrelated to this work and was true before it.
